I'm Dan Kiehl, an in-house compliance attorney in the Atlanta area. I build and run compliance programs for a defense engineering firm, covering:

- **Export controls:** ITAR, EAR, and DOE's 10 CFR Part 810
- **CMMC Level 2 and CUI:** program design, enclave scoping, assessment readiness
- **Industrial security:** facility clearances and FSO duties under NISPOM
- **Privacy:** U.S. and EU frameworks

I hold a JD from Valparaiso University School of Law and the CIPP/US, CIPP/E, and CIPM certifications.

I like building programs from nothing and figuring out how the rules actually work, not just what they say. This site is where I write that down.
