Most of what I know about export controls, CMMC, and industrial security I learned by building programs from scratch, often as the only compliance person in the room. The regulations are public. Knowing how they play out inside a real organization mostly isn't.

This site is my notebook: plain explanations of the rules, the questions I actually ask, and the tools I build to keep up. No hot takes, no "10 things every CEO must know." If something here helps you scope an enclave or spot an 810 issue earlier, it's done its job.
